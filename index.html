Super Civ

Your little city stands in the middle of a wild valley. You get 60 seconds of daylight to gather apples, mine gold and grab blue science shields before sunset. Raiders march in from both sides to raid your stores. Apples feed new citizens, gold builds, science researches. Carry your city from the Ancient era all the way to the Modern age and launch a rocket to the stars.

CollectApples ride in your basket (20 max). Hit rocks for gold, grab blue shields for science.
BankRun back through the city to store them safely.
DodgeA hit spills your basket. You have 3 hearts.
SunsetYou keep half of whatever is still in your basket.
